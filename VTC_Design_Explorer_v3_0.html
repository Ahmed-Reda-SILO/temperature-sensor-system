5. CINT exploration

Sweeps your configured capacitor range while keeping the recommended VTC gain fixed. This reveals the required IREF, kT/C noise, reset-switch resistance, leakage margin, and ramp-current power.

6. IREF exploration

Sweeps IREF using the recommended CINT. Regions are checked against minimum resolution, conversion-time, and leakage constraints.

7. VTC conversion-rate exploration

Sweeps aggregate VTC conversion rate. The recommended CINT, IREF, and clock are held fixed, while available conversion time and KVTC,max change.

Calculation basis

ΔVREQ = |ST| ΔTREQ   ΔVTEMP = |ST|(TMAX − TMIN)   VWIN = ΔVTEMP(1 + margin) unless manually overridden.

KVTC,max = tCONV,max / VWIN   KVTC,min = NREQ /(fCLK ΔVREQ)   KVTC = CINT / IREF.

VLSB = 1 /(fCLK KVTC)   TLSB = VLSB / |ST|   vn,C = √(kT/CINT).

The feasibility engine also checks aggregate channel throughput, reset/settling/guard overhead, IREF limits, and the user-defined leakage fraction. Power values shown are the charging-current contribution only, not total VTC power.
